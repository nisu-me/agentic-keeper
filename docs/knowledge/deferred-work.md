# Deferred work

The register of agreed-but-deferred work: deprecations in progress, pending adoptions, decisions parked, known debt. If the team said "we'll come back to this," it lives here — otherwise nobody comes back to it. Entry format: **what**, why deferred, what unblocks it, date — one unwrapped line per entry (rule 6 in [docs/README.md](../README.md)).
